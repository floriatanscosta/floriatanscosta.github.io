Design

Logo & Design

The design and visual identity of the logo were inspired by the classic representation of chemical elements in the periodic table. As a personal touch, the initials of my name make up the artwork, while the traditional atomic number was intentionally replaced by my date of birth.

For the layout, the palette features shades of blue and purple. These are my favourite colours, chosen to create a consistent visual identity that reflects my personality and my work both at the bench and in programming.

Palette

  • #5A729EPrimary blue
  • #24577FDeep blue
  • #666FE4Violet
  • #D13C86Magenta
  • #4CB96EGreen (highlights)
  • #1A1F26Dark background

Typography

Roboto

A single variable font, served by the site itself (no external dependencies). Generous line height for comfortable reading, headings in blue and technical data in a monospaced font.

Logo

A rounded square with a radial blue gradient, the letters “Fc” and the number 28 in the corner, like the cell of a chemical element.
