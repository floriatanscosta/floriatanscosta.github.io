Design

Logo & Design

The visual identity of this website comes from chemistry: the logo reinterprets the classic representation of an element in the periodic table.

The initials of my name take the place of the chemical symbol, and the atomic number was replaced by my date of birth. The palette brings together shades of blue and violet, my favourite colours, to give unity to everything here: from the lab bench to programming.

01

Logo

A periodic-table cell, with personal information in place of the element's properties.

  1. Corner number

    In the periodic table, this is where the atomic number goes. Here, 28 refers to my date of birth.

  2. Symbol

    The letters Fc, the initials of Floriatan Costa, sit where the chemical symbol would be, with the same prominence.

  3. Shape and colour

    A rounded square with a radial gradient from teal blue to deep blue; the letters are set in ice blue to keep the contrast.

Applications

  • Light background Full version, used in the site header.

  • Dark background The same version, unchanged: the square itself ensures legibility.

  • Browser icon The same design is used as the favicon and remains legible at small sizes.

Principles

  • One family

    Roboto for all text. As a variable font, a single file per style covers every weight, from thin to black.

  • Self-hosted

    The WOFF2 files are served by the site itself, with no external font services, which benefits privacy and performance.

  • Comfortable reading

    16 px body text, generous line height and controlled line length. Blue headings guide the reader.

  • Technical data

    Acronyms and codes use the system monospaced font, which makes similar characters easier to tell apart.
